I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Technology Engineering with a specialization in AI, as well as a Master's degree in Big Data Systems. Alongside my academic studies, I gained hands-on experience as an AI engineer at several technology startups. This experience sparked my interest in visualization and XAI, which became the central focus of both my Bachelor's and Master's theses.
</p>
